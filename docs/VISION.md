## The One-Liner

**Squabble Inn** is an audiobook book club app. Form a guild with your friends, pick a book, and see exactly where everyone is on the timeline. Leave Dark Souls-style comments that only appear after your guildmates pass that moment - no spoilers, just reactions. Earn themes styled like your favorite LitRPGs along the way.

---

## The Core Feature

**This is what Squabble IS:**

You and up to 5 friends form a guild (6 total). You pick a book to read together. As you listen, you can see exactly where everyone else is - ghost markers on the timeline showing their progress in real-time.

And you can leave comments.

**The Comment System (Dark Souls meets SoundCloud):**

When you hit a moment that makes you laugh, gasp, or want to scream - you drop a comment at that timestamp. But here's the key: **your guildmates don't see it until they pass that point.**

- You're at 2:34:17 and something crazy happens
- You leave a comment: "WHAT. NO. WHAT."
- Your friend is at 1:45:00 - they see nothing
- They reach 2:34:17 - your comment appears
- They add their own: "I KNOW RIGHT"
- The next person arrives and sees both

It's a trail of reactions. A breadcrumb path of emotions. Completely spoiler-free.

**This is the core loop:**
1. See where your guild is (ghost markers)
2. Read/listen at your pace
3. React to moments (comments appear after)
4. Experience your friends' reactions as you go
5. Discuss after everyone finishes

Everything else - themes, achievements, progression - enhances this core experience.

## Identity

### What is Squabble Inn?

Squabble Inn is not just an audiobook app - it's a *place*. When you open the app, you're stepping into your corner of the inn. Your reading history is displayed as trinkets on shelves. Your theme reflects the worlds you've conquered. Your guild occupies a table in the corner, and you can see exactly where everyone is in the current adventure.

### Why "Squabble"?

A squabble is a small, noisy argument - the kind that happens between adventuring party members about which way to go, who gets the loot, or whether to rest or push on. It's friendly conflict. It's banter. It's what happens when you and your friends are reading the same book and someone pulls ahead.

### Why "Inn"?

Every great adventure starts at an inn. It's where parties form, quests are posted, and stories are shared. The inn is a liminal space - between adventures, between worlds. Squabble Inn is where you gather before diving into the next book.

## Target Audience

### Primary: LitRPG & Progression Fantasy Listeners

These readers already think in our language:
- **Guilds** = parties, found family, adventure groups
- **Progress bars** = XP, leveling, advancement
- **Achievements** = system notifications, titles, ranks
- **Theming** = dungeon aesthetics, class identity, world-building

They consume content voraciously - often 50-100+ hours/month on Audible, Spotify, Royal Road, and Kindle Unlimited. They re-read favorites. They discuss in Discord servers and Reddit communities.

### Key Series/Touchstones
- Dungeon Crawler Carl (the gold standard for AI achievements)
- He Who Fights With Monsters
- Defiance of the Fall
- Cradle
- The Wandering Inn
- Primal Hunter
- Legends & Lattes (cozy subgenre)

### Why They'll Care
- **Visibility**: See where friends are in shared books
- **Identity**: App reflects their reading taste
- **Community**: Guild system built for their social patterns
- **Discovery**: Raid boss recommendations based on collective taste
- **Delight**: Easter eggs, achievements, hidden toys

## Core Pillars

### 1. The Inn (Your Space)

The app is a place, not just a tool. When you open Squabble, you're stepping into your personalized corner of the inn.

**Elements:**
- Your library as bookshelves
- Trinkets/trophies from completed books
- Theme reflecting your reading history
- Guild table with party members visible
- Bounty board for quests/challenges

**Key Insight:** The app should feel like opening a game you're excited to return to, not a utility you tolerate.

---

### 2. Earned Aesthetic

Nothing is just bought - it's *earned* through reading. Your app looks the way it does because of what you've accomplished.

**How Themes Unlock:**
- Complete a book → Unlock book-specific trinket
- Complete a series → Unlock series theme
- Guild completes together → Unlock guild-wide theme option
- Re-read a book → Enhanced trinket / special variant

**Theme Scope (Full Vision):**
- App icon
- Color palette
- Background artwork
- UI chrome and accents
- Sound effects (button taps, notifications)
- Player skin

**Example:**
> You've finished Dungeon Crawler Carl. Your inn can now be themed as the "Pineapple Cabaret" or "Carl & Donut's Safe Room." Clicking on the robot-cat-toy on your shelf plays a Donut-like quip.

---

### 3. Dual Progression

Progress is tracked at two levels, creating interplay between personal and social achievement.

**Personal Progression:**
- Books completed
- Series completed
- Re-reads tracked
- Achievements unlocked
- Class identity (see Wild Ideas)

**Guild Progression:**
- Collective book completion (all 6 members finished)
- Series milestones
- Shared book completions
- Raid boss completions
- Shared theme unlocks

**Key Insight:** Personal progress feeds guild progress. Guild goals motivate personal reading.

---

### 4. The Bounty Board

A central hub for quests, challenges, and goals - framed as a literal bounty board in an inn.

**Quest Types:**
- **Personal Bounties**: Finish this book, hit 50 hours this month
- **Guild Bounties**: Everyone finish this series, complete a book together
- **Raid Bosses**: Guild-recommended challenge based on class composition
- **Seasonal Events**: Community-wide reading challenges
- **Royal Road Serials**: Ongoing quests that update with new chapters

**Framing:** You're not just reading - you're completing quests. The language matters.

## The Pitch

### Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)

> Squabble Inn is an audiobook app built for LitRPG fans. You form a guild with up to 5 friends (6 total), and you can see exactly where everyone is in the books you're reading together - like ghost markers on the timeline. Complete books and series to unlock themes from your favorite worlds. Your app can look like Dungeon Crawler Carl's safe room or the Wandering Inn's common room. It's an audiobook club with progression mechanics, achievements, and tons of easter eggs.

### Why Now?

- LitRPG is the fastest-growing fantasy subgenre
- Audible's social features are nonexistent
- BookPlayer is a mature open-source foundation
- Discord communities prove the audience wants to connect around books
- No one is building specifically for this audience

## Monetization Philosophy

### Principles

1. **Core experience is free** - Playback, library, joining guilds, basic features
2. **Delight is optional** - Cosmetic progression for those who want it
3. **Creation has value** - Starting a guild is a one-time investment (includes delight features)
4. **Aspirational tier** - Future social features for the most dedicated adventurers

### Tier Structure

| Tier | Cost | Access |
|------|------|--------|
| **Traveler** (Free) | $0 | Full playback, join guilds, ghost markers, comments, basic themes |
| **Resident Adventurer** | One-time ~$5-7 | Achievements, premium themes, trinkets, bounty board, class identity |
| **Guild Master** | One-time ~$10-15 | Everything in Resident Adventurer + create/manage guilds |
| **Ascendant Adventurer** | TBD | Global social features, cross-guild connections ("the multiverse") |

### Why This Works

- **Low barrier**: Anyone can use the app and join friends for free
- **One-time purchases**: No subscription fatigue - pay once, own forever
- **Guild investment**: Creator is committed (and gets all delight features), prevents spam guilds
- **Aspirational tier**: Future expansion for power users who want broader social connections

## Design Philosophy

### 1. Earned, Not Bought

Themes, trinkets, and achievements come from reading - not from a store. You can't skip to the DCC theme without reading DCC. This creates genuine meaning.

### 2. DCC-Style AI Voice

Achievements and system messages should feel like the Dungeon's AI from Dungeon Crawler Carl - sardonic, clever, occasionally insulting, always entertaining.

**Examples:**
> **Achievement Unlocked: "Speed Reader or Liar?"**
> *You finished a 40-hour audiobook in 6 hours at 3x speed. Your comprehension is... questionable.*

> **Achievement Unlocked: "The Wandering Listener"**
> *You've started 15 books this month and finished 2. Commitment issues?*

> **Achievement Unlocked: "Completionist"**
> *You've finished every book in a 12-book series. The dungeon acknowledges your dedication. Barely.*

### 3. Easter Eggs Everywhere

Hidden interactions reward curiosity:
- Click the robot cat trinket → Donut-like quip
- Tap the inn sign 5 times → Secret sound
- Complete a specific combo → Hidden achievement
- Theme elements have interactive moments

### 4. Community First

Features prioritize connection over isolation:
- Ghost markers make reading social
- Guild progression creates shared goals
- In-progress comments enable async discussion
- Shared completion creates celebration moments

### 5. Respect the Source Material

When implementing themes from specific books:
- Honor the aesthetic and tone
- Reach out to authors/artists when possible
- Provide templates for official collaboration
- Credit sources prominently

## Open Questions

### Product Questions

1. **Guild size**: Base is 6 members. Should Ascendant Adventurer unlock larger guilds (8-10)?

2. **Re-reads**: How do we make re-reading feel rewarding? Enhanced trinkets? Tracking counts? Special achievements?

3. **Completion definition**: What % counts as "complete"? 95%? 100%? User-marked?

4. **Onboarding**: How do we explain the guild system to new users without overwhelming?

5. **Solo users**: Is the app compelling for someone without a guild? Should we have public guilds or guild discovery?

### Technical Questions

1. **Theme asset pipeline**: How do we scale theme creation? Templates? User-generated?

2. **Audio clips licensing**: Can we get permissions for book-specific sounds?

3. **Ascendant Adventurer features**: What global social features make sense? Public guilds? Cross-guild events?

### Business Questions

1. **Pricing research**: What would LitRPG listeners pay? Survey the Discord communities.

2. **Author partnerships**: Who do we approach first? Most engaged with community?

3. **Marketing channels**: r/litrpg, r/ProgressionFantasy, author newsletters, podcast sponsors?
